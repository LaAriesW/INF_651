HOW TO WIN MORE GAMES

    Combat Strategy

  • Stick with a weapon and equipment combo that matches your playstyle. Experiment during practice matches to find what feels natural—whether it’s close-range SMG rushes or precise sniper play.
  • Always move from cover to cover during engagements. Avoid sprinting in open areas, and use crouch or prone mechanics to make yourself a smaller target.
  • Don’t engage enemies on their terms. Use flashbangs, smoke, or tactical grenades to create favorable situations, and retreat if you're outnumbered to regroup with teammates.

    • Map Awareness

  • Focus on mastering choke points and high-traffic zones on each map. Knowing where players commonly appear gives you a tactical edge.
  • Look for ledges, stairways, and vantage points that provide a superior angle on enemies. Many players neglect to check upward during firefights.
  • Wear a good headset and listen carefully for footsteps, reload sounds, or enemy callouts. This information can alert you to incoming threats or nearby skirmishes.
