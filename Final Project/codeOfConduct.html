CODE OF CONDUCT

    Respect All Players

  • Treat all players with respect, regardless of skill level, nationality, gender, race, or any other characteristic.
  • Avoid toxic behavior such as verbal abuse, harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
  • Encourage and support your teammates during gameplay.

    • Play Fair

  • Do not exploit bugs, glitches, or use unauthorized third-party software to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Report any exploits or cheating you observe to the game’s support team.
  • Follow the rules of the game and compete honestly.

    • Communicate Constructively

  • Use in-game communication channels responsibly. Avoid spamming, trolling, or using offensive language.
  • Be a positive force in the community by offering constructive feedback and helping new players.
