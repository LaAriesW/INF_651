PATCH NOTES:

    Bug Fixes

  • Resolved a bug where some players experienced inconsistent hit detection, particularly in high-latency matches.
  • Fixed instances of invisible walls blocking player movement on several maps, especially in "Nightfall Facility" and "Desert Ruins."
  • Adjusted 3D audio to ensure accurate positioning of enemy footsteps and gunfire sounds, improving player awareness.

    • Weapon Balances

  • Increased vertical recoil slightly to make mid-range engagements more challenging while retaining close-quarters effectiveness.
  • Decreased the reload time by 15% and increased body shot damage by 10%, making it a more viable choice in fast-paced scenarios.
  • Reduced pellet spread tightness and decreased damage at long ranges to ensure better balance against other weapons in mid-range combat.
